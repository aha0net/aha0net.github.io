zeste privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

zeste is an iPhone app that adds your voice to your photos and videos and makes a clip you can save and share. It is made by aha0.

What zeste collects

Nothing. zeste has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your photos, videos, voice or anything else to us or to anyone.

What stays on your device

The photos and videos you choose, the voice you record and the clips zeste makes are kept in the app's own storage on your device while you work on them. When you save a finished clip to Photos, zeste removes its working files. Deleting zeste work never deletes your original photos or videos. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept.

Permissions zeste asks for

Photos, to show your photos and videos and to save finished clips. Microphone, to record your voice. Notifications, optional, to tell you when a clip is ready. Notifications are created on your device and go through no server of ours.

Purchases

After five saved clips, zeste offers a one-time unlock bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. zeste only learns from Apple whether the unlock is owned. We never see your payment details.

Children

zeste collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because zeste keeps no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have a question about privacy, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.
