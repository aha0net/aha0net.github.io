Help with the app, and how to reach us.

zeste is an iPhone app that adds your voice to your photos and videos and makes a clip you can save to Photos and share. It is made by aha0. Everything happens on your phone; there is no account.

How it works

Tap New clip and choose photos and videos. Tap Next, then Record, and talk while you swipe through them. Pause and resume if you need to. Tap Finish, watch the clip, then tap Save and share.

Where do my clips go?

A finished clip is saved to your Photos library when you tap Save and share. Until then it waits under In progress on the home screen. Saving a clip removes it from In progress; your original photos and videos are never changed or removed.

Can I add more photos to a clip?

Yes, as long as the clip is waiting for your voice or a take is paused. Open the clip and tap the plus tile at the end of the film strip, or choose more from the picker. Photos and videos already in the clip show a check and cannot be added twice.

What is free and what costs money?

Your first five saved clips are free and end with a short zeste card. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited clips with no end card. There is no subscription and nothing recurring. The price is shown in your local currency on the unlock sheet, under the menu at the top right or on Record once the free clips are used.

I bought the unlock on another phone, or reinstalled the app

Open the menu at the top right, choose Unlock zeste, then tap Restore purchases. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account and comes back on any iPhone signed in with it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and find zeste.

Why is the video silent while I record?

Videos play muted while you record so your microphone does not pick up an echo. The finished clip still has their sound, lowered under your voice. To hear a video while you talk, wear headphones and use the headphones control on the recording screen. For the clearest voice, record somewhere quiet.

Which iPhones and languages?

zeste runs on iPhone with iOS 16 or later. It is available in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Dutch and follows your phone's language setting.

Privacy

zeste collects nothing. Photos, videos, voice and clips stay on your phone. The full privacy policy is short.
